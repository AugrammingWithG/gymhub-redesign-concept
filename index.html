FAQs

Why is the 90-day gym growth plan free?

Steve wants to meet you, learn about your gym and find what’s missing or holding you back. You finish the call with a 3-step, 90-day action plan. If you’re not a fit for one-on-one mentoring, you still keep the plan and the framework.

Will Steve try to sell me anything?

No. The call is about your gym and your plan.

What is GymHub mentoring?

An application-only mentoring program for gym owners. The goal is at least $100,000 in added 12-month sales, plus the team and systems to take Fridays off. Members work with Steve every week.

When is mentoring right for me?

You own a gym in Australia, New Zealand or the USA, you’re already making around $4K a month, you can add 50 members in 90 days, and you’ll spend about 5 hours a week on the strategies.

How does the application work?

A 1-minute survey with a few quick questions about your business. It filters out non-owners and casual enquiries, so good fits can book a free call with Steve on Zoom.

What experience does Steve have?

23 years in fitness in every role from PT and group fitness to sales and management. He lectured at university for three years, has built two gyms past $1 million in turnover, and has mentored more than 100 fitness brands.
